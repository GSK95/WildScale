Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

WildScale is a plugin for Adobe Premiere Pro made by Drugela. This page explains what happens to your data.

Your footage

WildScale analyzes frames on your own computer. A small service runs on your machine and only accepts connections from that same machine. Your footage, frames, sequences and project files are not uploaded to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your computer

  • Marks you choose to remember, including the frames they came from
  • Fixes and references you choose to remember
  • A low-resolution motion scan of videos you process
  • Temporary frames used during analysis

These files stay in your local app data folder. You can clear marks and fixes in the Memory card, and you can delete the folder at any time. Uninstalling WildScale does not remove it, so delete it by hand if you want it gone.

What we collect

WildScale has no account system, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect personal data through the plugin.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses to run the service, as described in its own privacy policy. We do not add analytics or tracking to this site.

Adobe

If you get WildScale through Adobe, Adobe handles that distribution under its own terms and privacy policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
